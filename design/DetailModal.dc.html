Committee of the Whole - Standing

Draft boundaries set for the new Seaton elementary school

Affects: Pickering Ajax

Executive summary

  1. 1 Trustees reviewed draft attendance boundaries for the new Seaton-area elementary school opening September 2027.
  2. 2 About 640 students at three nearby Pickering and Ajax schools would move to the new school.
  3. 3 Public feedback is open until October 9; a final vote is expected October 20.

What this means for students & parents

If you live north of Taunton Road in Pickering, or in west Ajax near the Pickering border, your child's home school may change in September 2027. Students now in Grade 7 could finish Grade 8 at their current school, and younger siblings could stay with them for one transition year if families ask. Bus eligibility would be recalculated from the new school, so some families who walk today may qualify for busing, and some who ride may not. The consultation survey, open until October 9, is the only formal chance to comment before trustees vote on October 20.

Policy changes

No board policy is being rewritten. The draft updates the elementary attendance boundary map under the board's boundary review procedure, and adds a one-year grandparenting rule for current Grade 7 students and their siblings.
