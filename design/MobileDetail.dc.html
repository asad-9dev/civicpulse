Draft boundaries set for the new Seaton elementary school

Pickering Ajax

Executive summary

1 Trustees reviewed draft attendance boundaries for the new Seaton-area elementary school opening September 2027.
2 About 640 students at three nearby Pickering and Ajax schools would move to the new school.
3 Public feedback is open until October 9; a final vote is expected October 20.

What this means for students & parents

If you live north of Taunton Road in Pickering, or in west Ajax near the Pickering border, your child's home school may change in September 2027. Students now in Grade 7 could finish Grade 8 at their current school.
